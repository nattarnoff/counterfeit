Building a Program

Move from component-level implementation into legal, conformance, compliance, reporting, and ACR/VPAT planning.

Legal and policy

Document the commitments, ownership, and decision process that govern accessibility work.

Conformance

Map components and journeys to WCAG requirements and keep evidence with each decision.

Compliance

Track remediation work, release readiness, and exceptions in a repeatable workflow.

Reporting

Keep a public or internal channel for accessibility issues and acknowledge submissions promptly.

ACRs and VPATs

Capture known support levels, testing scope, and exceptions in current documentation artifacts.
